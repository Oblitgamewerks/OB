At Oblit Gamewerks, our design philosophy is straightforward: strip away modern padding, respect the player’s time & deliver raw, unadulterated FPS gameplay through a distinct retro-tactical aesthetic and heavily modified engine architecture. Our games aren’t for everyone and you will receive no hand holding. You will be dumped into a level and you're expected to figure it out yourself. No safe spaces provided: you can’t hide behind rubble for 5 seconds to heal your bullet wounds. Bullets have consequences.Cut scenes are cut: Our games are for players who want to shoot baddies, not watch a movie! Our signature war room launcher grants players absolute freedom of access by avoiding artificial progression gates and leaving every level unlocked from the very first launch. You paid for it, you own it. You should be allowed to play whatever and whenever you want! While non-sequential design offers immediate access to any operational zone, the campaign is explicitly structured to be experienced sequentially; the progression escalates naturally. If you choose to bypass the early deployments and jump straight into the bullet ridden hell of the advanced levels, only to get wiped out quickly, that's on you, war is hell.
