We are absolutely thrilled to announce that the worldwide launch of our game

Witnessing the explosive response from players across the globe has been nothing short of surreal.

The sheer volume of people who jumped into the game on day one completely blew past
our highest forecasts and loftiest expectations. We are still riding a massive wave of

initial results, and we want to say a massive thank you to everyone who clicked 'Play.

Your early feedback, enthusiasm, and bugs reported during the fest were instrumental in helping us
